Hello there! We are so excited that you will be joining us for our workshop on getting started with Positron at useR! 2025 in person on the Duke University campus on Aug 8.

We have setup instructions at https://posit-dev.github.io/positron-workshop/setup.html, but there are a few details for you to think about ahead of our time together at the workshop:

- Please install Positron ahead of the workshop, on the laptop that you plan to bring with you. Get the latest version at https://positron.posit.co/download.
- You will also need R installed on your laptop, as Positron does not bundle R (or Python). You will need R >= 4.2; follow the instructions for your operating system at https://cloud.r-project.org/.
- Once you have Positron and R installed, open it to make sure the installation has gone well and Positron can find your R installation. Confirm that you can run `1 + 1` in Positron's R console.
- If you have trouble installing Positron or discovering R, please post about the challenges you are facing at https://github.com/posit-dev/positron/discussions; we'll try to get you unstuck before we are together in person at the workshop.

We are looking forward to sharing more about how to use Positron with R!
